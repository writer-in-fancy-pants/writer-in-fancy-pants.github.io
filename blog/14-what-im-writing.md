Like so many times before, I have started writing fiction again. I haven't pinned down where the urge stems from, though excessive reading or reality beyond my control often seem to be associated with the impetus.

I am writing two stories. Both of them open-ended, about tropes quite common in recent fantasy fiction. They are part of a bigger world that sort-of exists in my head.

## Breach
(https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/171254/breach)[https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/171254/breach]
Urban dungeons. What would happen if there were suddenly pocket dimensions with insanely large wonderous regions available for exploration. Except, the exploration has been forced upon some of the participants due to circumstances.

This idea has called to me for a while. I've read quite a few stories like this, but I find the use of system interface distasteful, as well as the overly straightforward nature of dungeons that leads to mob grinding, and not natural discovery of a completely new landscape. It feels like a natural human urge, to have an accessible yet dangerous frontier, more visceral than cerebral.

## Kaleidoscope
(https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/171921/kaleidoscope)[https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/171921/kaleidoscope]
Transmigration. Reconciling with a new life. Except, not too hacky or overpowered. More slice of life. Discovery. Intrigue. Exploring philosophical questions as they arise - indentity, morality, purpose of life, etc.

The story revolves around finding oneself in an alien world, a place where the familiar map is not easily available. The various characters are like us, yet different, and how the one person from modern earth now unmoored of his chosen bindings navigates his life. It is a slow-burn, as much an exploration for me as I wish it is for the readers.
